# 🐿️ Nakasendo Dynamic Route Planner

This is a trip planner for the Walk Japan Self-Guided Nakasendo Wayfarer, 13–19 Nov 2026. It shows:

- where you'll be at any time of day;
- where you can duck out by bus or train, and when the next two departures are;
- whether turning back or pressing on is quicker;
- which stretches have no way out.

**Live map:** https://raxter.github.io/nakasendo-dynamic-route-planner/. Tap **🗺️** for a real map with your blue dot. Tap the dot, then **Set position & time to here**.

**Sketch-map version (Claude artifact):** https://claude.ai/artifact/UDCorA8nSLtETrTVqudNxi

**Printable guides:** `docs/Nakasendo_trail_duckouts_EN.pdf` (English) and `docs/Nakasendo_trail_duckouts_EN-JA.pdf` (English | 日本語, with phrases to show staff).

Times marked ≈ are estimates. Check locally and follow your Walk Japan notes where they differ.
